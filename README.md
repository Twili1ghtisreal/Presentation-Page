# 💚 Twilight.dev

A little corner of the internet for everything I make and play — my DDLC mods, the
cheaters I catch in Left 4 Dead 2, the community cheater database, my Steam library,
and whatever else I'm up to.

It's styled like a literature club journal: chapters instead of sections, a typewriter
line at the top, petals drifting down, a light and dark mode, and a green *Just Monika*
theme if pink isn't your thing.

**Live page → https://twili1ghtisreal.github.io/Presentation-Page/**

## What's on it

**Videos** — my YouTube channel is all Left 4 Dead 2: catching cheaters on camera and
posting the receipts. Short clips go to TikTok, community reports and updates to Reddit.

**Mods** — *The Outside*, my first mod: a short drama demo I rushed to finish and
release (discontinued, but an episode 2 is coming). And *The Outside: Remastered*, the
rebuilt version with a new prologue, six chapters, multiple endings, chapter select,
achievements and a memory gallery.

**Projects** — the L4D2 Cheater Database: cheaters caught by the community, with the
proof attached and a way to report more.

**Steam** — what I'm playing, and the two games that have eaten the most of my time.

**Discord** — a small Literature Club server for mod previews, video feedback and
hanging out. Opening soon.

**Poem** — a shuffled club notebook. Hit the button and it deals you a random poem
written in one of the girls' voices, in her colour.

## How it's put together

The whole page is one `index.html` — HTML, CSS and JavaScript in a single file. No
frameworks, no build step, no dependencies. Picture slots fall back to local
placeholders if a remote image ever fails, and the music panel copes with missing files
instead of breaking.

There's no analytics and no cookies. The only things it remembers are your own choices,
kept in your browser: theme, music volume, and how many poems you've read.
